How it works

Three steps to free up space

1

Hold a photo

Hold a photo you don’t need, then tap others. They gather into a stack.

2

Drop the stack in a container

Drag the stack into the jar, the box or the bucket. Or just tap the container you want.

3

Look inside and delete

Open a container, mark what to keep and tap “Delete the rest”. You can usually restore deleted photos for 30 days.

Nostalgia

Remember that summer?

Pick “Oldest first” in Sort and scroll back through the years. To keep new photos out of the way, use “Show photos” in Settings.

Sound and feel

You hear it and feel it in your hand

Each container has its own sound. The jar rings, the box thuds, the bag rustles, and your phone gives a soft tap in your palm. The sounds are real recordings, no AI. Just a pantry, containers and beans.

Result

See how much space you freed

After each deletion, Pelusa shows how much space you freed up and celebrates with you. Stats add it all up by week.

You can usually restore deleted photos for 30 days. After that, they’re erased permanently.

Android: deleted photos go to your phone’s trash. Restore them right in Pelusa, on the Trash screen.

iPhone: deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and stay there for 30 days. Pelusa keeps a history of what was deleted, with a button that opens Photos.

Your photos, your call

Your photos stay on your phone

Only you decide

Questions

Why does Pelusa need access to my photos?

To show your gallery as a feed. Pelusa shows photos right from your gallery and never sends them anywhere. On iPhone and Android 14+ you can allow access to selected photos only, and Pelusa will work with those. Nothing is deleted until you confirm in your phone’s system dialog.

Does Pelusa delete photos on its own?

No. Photos are deleted only after you confirm in the phone’s system dialog. And even then you can restore them for 30 days. On rare Android phones without a trash, deleting is permanent right away. Pelusa tells you about it after deleting.

How do I restore deleted photos?

On Android, right in Pelusa, on the Trash screen. On iPhone, in Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where photos are kept for 30 days.

Will a photo be deleted from iCloud or Google Photos?

iPhone: if iCloud Photos is on, deleting removes the photo from all your devices and from iCloud. This is how iPhone works. After 30 days, the photo is deleted permanently.

Android: Pelusa deletes photos only from the phone. A copy in Google Photos, if there is one, stays.

Do I need an internet connection?

No, Pelusa works offline. Try it in airplane mode. The network is only used for crash reports and stats (you can turn them off in Settings) and for support purchases. On iPhone with iCloud Photos, the system may download a photo from iCloud when you open it full screen.

Will Pelusa find duplicates and bad photos for me?

No, and that’s on purpose. No AI decides for you what matters. You just scroll, remember and drop what you don’t need into a container.

What do the containers mean?

Whatever you like. Containers have no labels: screenshots go in the jar, receipts in the box, “I’ll sort it later” in the bucket. Each one sounds different, so they’re easy to tell apart.

Why is it free?

I made Pelusa for my family, and I’d like it to help others too. There are no ads or subscriptions. If you’d like to say thanks, you can support the author in Settings.
