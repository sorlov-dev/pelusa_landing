3. What Pelusa is

Pelusa helps you tidy up your phone’s gallery: view your photos as a feed, gather the ones you don’t need into a stack, drop them into containers and delete them through the phone’s system dialog.

Pelusa is free, with no ads and no subscriptions. Pelusa has no accounts: you don’t sign up or log in.

4. Deleting photos: only when you decide

  • Pelusa never deletes photos on its own. When you delete photos from a container, Pelusa asks the system to delete them, and the system deletes them only after you confirm in its own dialog.
  • On iPhone, deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and can be restored there for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is on, a deleted photo is also removed from your other devices and from iCloud.
  • On most Android phones, deleted photos go to the system trash and can usually be restored for about 30 days, including right in Pelusa. Some Android phones have no trash, and the system deletes photos right away and permanently. Pelusa can’t know this in advance: it checks after the first deletion and, if there is no trash, says so on the results screen.
  • When the restore period ends, the system deletes photos permanently, and neither we nor the App can bring them back.
  • You choose which photos to delete. Please look carefully before confirming. To the extent permitted by law, we are not responsible for deleting photos you confirmed or for its consequences.

5. Your photos and backups

Containers, Pelusa’s trash history and settings are stored only on your device, and we can’t view or recover them. If you uninstall the App, clear its data or lose your phone, this information may be lost. If photos matter to you, keep a separate copy.
